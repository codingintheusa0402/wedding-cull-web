Free culling for wedding, snap and studio photographers

Wedding Cull flags the frames you would delete: blur and camera shake, missed focus, closed eyes, too dark or too bright, lens-cap misfires. It picks the best shot of every burst. You confirm in a few minutes, then open the result in Lightroom Classic, Adobe Bridge or Photoshop.

or drop the folder here · JPEG and RAW (ARW, CR3, CR2, NEF, DNG, RAF, ORF, RW2)

1

Choose the folder

Straight from the SD card or a copy. RAW files are read through their embedded preview, so it is fast.

2

Check the flagged ones

Only rejects and "check" photos are shown, grouped by reason, each burst with its best frame starred.

3

Open in your editor

Ratings, labels and keywords are written as standard XMP: Lightroom, Bridge and Photoshop read them.

Open source on GitHub · MIT license · Tested on Mac: Chrome saves straight into the folder, Safari downloads a ZIP. Firefox and Windows: testing soon.
